What the numbers mean

Impressions

Times Amazon displayed your advert, not unique people.

Clicks and CTR

Clicks are visits; CTR is clicks per 100 impressions. It indicates interest.

CPC

Average advertising cost per click. A click is not a purchase.

Sales

Sales credited to ads, not total KDP sales. Recent attribution can lag.

ACoS

Ad spend ÷ ad-attributed sales × 100. £2 spent to create £10 sales = 20% ACoS.

Profit

Retail price isn't your profit. We need the KDP royalty per sale to assess payback.
