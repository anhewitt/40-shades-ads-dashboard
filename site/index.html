What the metrics mean, in plain English

Impressions

How many times an advert appeared on Amazon. Not unique people.

Clicks / CTR

Clicks are people tapping your advert. CTR is clicks per 100 times shown.

CPC

Cost per click: how much you pay, on average, for someone to visit the listing.

Conversion rate

Ad-attributed orders divided by ad clicks. Only meaningful once there's enough traffic.

ACoS

Ad spend divided by sales revenue attributed to adverts. A lower figure is usually better, but it isn't profit.

Estimated ad contribution

Attributed copies × £2.64 royalty, minus ad spend. Doesn't include organic sales or other business expenses.

Amazon Ads UK figures via Windsor.ai, published through GitHub Actions. The £2.64 royalty per paperback is your approximate estimate, applied equally to both titles. Attribution can lag up to 14 days. These are ad-attributed sales only, not total KDP sales. Missing data is shown as unavailable, never assumed to be zero. This website is publicly accessible.
